## Key features

- **Zero trading fees** — No commissions on any trade
- **Instant onboarding** — Sign in with wallet, email, Twitter, or Discord via Privy
- **Permissionless markets** — Anyone can create a market via Attest — no gatekeepers
- **Social trading** — Compete in Pulses, climb leaderboards, and pool capital in Funds with friends
- **Gamified rewards** — Earn points through streaks, referrals, and volume — unlock badges and multipliers
- **Gasless trading** — Noma covers gas fees on Polygon
- **Multi-source aggregation** — Polymarket, Kalshi, and more in one place
- **Portfolio tracking** — Unified view of all your positions across providers
