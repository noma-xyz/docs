One interface for every prediction market. Polymarket, Kalshi, and more — with zero trading fees, best-price routing, and a public identity that tracks every call you make.

Noma compares orderbooks across venues and routes you to the best price automatically. Your Noma ID ties it all together: win rate, badges, streaks, and leaderboard ranking — one track record across every provider, verifiable by anyone.

<Tip>
On other aggregator platforms, every trade costs you 1–2% in platform fees. noma charges **zero platform fees** and covers gas on Polygon and Solana — what you see is what you pay.
</Tip>
