## Getting crypto (if you don't have any)

If you're new to crypto and don't already hold USDC, here's the quickest path:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an exchange account">
    Sign up for a centralized exchange such as
    [Coinbase](https://www.coinbase.com), [Kraken](https://www.kraken.com), or
    [Binance](https://www.binance.com). You'll need to verify your identity
    (KYC) before you can buy or withdraw crypto.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deposit fiat currency">
    Link a bank account, debit card, or other payment method and deposit USD (or
    your local currency) into your exchange account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Buy USDC">
    Purchase **USDC** on the exchange. USDC is a stablecoin pegged 1:1 to the US
    dollar, so 1 USDC ≈ $1.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send to your wallet">
    Withdraw USDC from the exchange to a self-custody wallet like **MetaMask**
    or **Coinbase Wallet**. Make sure you select the correct network (Polygon
    for USDC.e, Solana for USDC, or any supported chain if you plan to use Blink
    — see below).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If you already have USDC in a wallet, skip straight to the deposit methods
  below.
</Tip>

### Depositing on Polygon

Polygon deposits go to your **Trading Safe** — the smart contract wallet that holds your trading funds and executes trades.

<Warning>
  If you haven't set up your Trading Safe yet, noma will prompt you to complete
  the one-time [wallet setup](/wallet/setup) first. It takes 30 seconds and is
  completely free.
</Warning>

#### Methods

1. **QR code** — Scan with any wallet app to send USDC.e directly to your Safe.
2. **Direct transfer** — Copy your Safe address and send USDC.e from any Polygon wallet.
3. **Coinbase Onramp** — Buy USDC with a debit card or bank transfer directly within the deposit modal.
4. **Blink** — Connect your browser extension wallet (MetaMask, Coinbase Wallet, etc.) and Blink automatically bridges your funds to the correct chain. For example, you can send USDC from **Base** and receive **USDC.e on Polygon** — no manual bridging required.

<Frame>
  <img
    src="/images/deposits-modal.jpg"
    alt="Deposit modal showing chain selection (Polygon USDC.e or Solana USDC) and amount input"
  />
</Frame>

#### Important

- Send **USDC.e** (bridged USDC), not native USDC. Contract: `0x2791Bca1f2de4661ED88A30C99A7a9449Aa84174`
- Send to your **Safe address**, not your embedded wallet address. Your Safe address is shown in the deposit modal.
- Deposits typically confirm in under 30 seconds on Polygon.

## Depositing with Blink

Blink is the easiest way to deposit if you already hold crypto in a browser extension wallet on any supported chain.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the deposit modal">
    Click **Deposit** on the dashboard and select **Blink**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your wallet">
    Connect your browser extension wallet (MetaMask, Coinbase Wallet, or any
    compatible wallet).
    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/images/deposit-blink-wallet-connect.jpg"
        alt="Blink deposit flow showing wallet connect screen with wallet options"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose amount and confirm">
    Enter the amount of USDC you'd like to deposit. Blink handles all bridging
    automatically — for example, USDC on Base is converted to USDC.e on Polygon
    behind the scenes.
    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/images/deposit-blink-coin-selection.jpg"
        alt="Blink deposit flow showing coin selection screen with available options"
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Blink supports bridging from multiple chains. You don't need to manually
  bridge or swap tokens before depositing.
</Note>

## Checking your balance

Your balance is shown in the top-right corner of the app. Click it to see your Safe address (Polygon), Solana wallet, and quick access to the deposit modal.

<Note>
  Balance updates may take a few seconds after a deposit confirms on-chain.
</Note>
