## Supported networks

| Network | Token | Use |
|---------|-------|-----|
| **Polygon** | USDC.e (bridged USDC) | Polymarket-powered prediction markets |
| **Solana** | SOL | Solana-based markets and future Kalshi integration |

## Depositing on Polygon

Polygon deposits go to your **Trading Safe** — a smart contract wallet that holds your trading funds.

<Warning>
If you haven't set up your Trading Safe yet, Nomawill prompt you to complete the one-time [wallet setup](/wallet/setup) before you can deposit.
</Warning>

### Methods

1. **QR code** — Scan with any wallet app to send USDC.e directly to your Safe
2. **Direct transfer** — Copy your Safe address and send USDC.e from any Polygon wallet
3. **On-ramp** — Buy USDC with a debit card or bank transfer via MoonPay (built into the deposit modal)

### Important notes

- Send **USDC.e** (bridged USDC), not native USDC. The contract address is `0x2791Bca1f2de4661ED88A30C99A7a9449Aa84174`
- Send to your **Safe address**, not your embedded wallet address. Your Safe address is shown in the deposit modal and the balance tooltip
- Deposits typically confirm in under 30 seconds on Polygon

## Depositing on Solana

Solana deposits go directly to your embedded Solana wallet address. Use the deposit modal to view your address or QR code.

## Checking your balance

Your USDC.e balance is shown in the top-right corner of the app. Click it to see:
- Your current balance
- Your Safe address (Polygon)
- Quick access to the deposit modal

<Note>
Balance updates may take a few seconds after a deposit confirms on-chain.
</Note>
