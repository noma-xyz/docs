## Wallet architecture

noma uses a two-wallet system for secure, gasless trading on Polygon:

| Wallet | Purpose |
|--------|---------|
| **Embedded wallet (EOA)** | Your main wallet, created automatically when you sign up. Holds your keys. |
| **Trading Safe** | A smart contract wallet (Gnosis Safe) deployed on Polygon. Holds your trading funds and executes trades. |

The Safe is controlled by your embedded wallet — only you can authorize transactions.

## First-time setup

When you first deposit to Polygon or place a trade, Nomawalks you through a one-time setup:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Deploy Safe">
    A Gnosis Safe contract is deployed on Polygon for your account. You'll sign a message to authorize the deployment. This is gasless — Nomacovers the transaction fees.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set approvals">
    The Safe needs permission to interact with the prediction market contracts (CTF Exchange). A batch of approval transactions is signed and relayed. Also gasless.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate trading credentials">
    API credentials are derived for your wallet to authenticate with the Polymarket CLOB (order matching engine). You sign an EIP-712 message, and Nomasecurely stores the encrypted credentials on the backend.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Safe address">
    Your Safe address is linked to your Nomaprofile. All future Polygon deposits go to this address.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The entire process takes about 30 seconds and requires 2–3 signature prompts (no gas fees).

## Security

- Your private keys **never leave your device** — Nomauses Privy's embedded wallet infrastructure
- Trading credentials are **encrypted at rest** (AES-256-GCM) on noma's backend
- The Safe contract is **non-custodial** — only your EOA can authorize transactions
- All relay transactions are **signed by you** before submission
