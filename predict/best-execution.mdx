Noma pulls live orderbook data from every connected venue, compares prices in real time, and puts you on the one with the best fill. No stale quotes, no cached prices — just the current best ask and best bid, side by side.

## How it works

1. You open a market from the **Predict** page
2. Noma checks which providers list it (via the [matching engine](/predict/cross-provider-matching))
3. It compares live best ask (for buys) and best bid (for sells) across venues
4. The trading panel opens on the venue with the better price

You pay **zero trading fees** on every venue. The only cost is the spread.

## What "best" means

| Factor | What noma checks |
|--------|-----------------|
| **Price** | Which venue gives you the most shares per dollar |
| **Depth** | Whether the book can fill your full size without walking the price |
| **Spread** | Tighter spread = less cost to get in and out |

For most orders, the venue with the better top-of-book price wins. For larger sizes, depth matters more — a slightly worse price with real liquidity beats a great quote that can only fill a fraction of your order.

## Smart routing

Noma picks the best venue for you by default. You can override it at any time.

### Auto-routing

Every time you navigate to a matched market — from the Predict page, a selector pill, a chart, or a shared link — noma evaluates both venues and selects one:

1. **Where you have funds** — If you only hold USDC on one chain (say Solana but not Polygon), noma routes to the venue where you can actually trade. No point landing on a venue with a zero balance.
2. **Where the price is better** — If you have funds on both chains (or neither), noma picks the venue with the lower ask.

<Tip>
Auto-routing runs fresh every time you navigate. Switch to a different market within an event and it re-evaluates — you always land on the best venue for *that* market, not just the one you were on before.
</Tip>

### Better price prompt

About to buy on the more expensive venue? Noma will catch it.

If the other provider has a better price by **2 cents or more**, a prompt appears before your order goes through. Two options:

- **Switch** — Jump to the cheaper venue
- **Continue** — Place the order where you are

This comparison runs against **live orderbook data**, not stored prices. And it only fires on buys — sells require holding shares or contracts on the specific provider, so switching venues isn't an option.

### Manual override

Click the **Polymarket** or **Kalshi** tab in the trading panel to lock in your choice. Once you pick a provider manually, noma respects it — auto-routing won't override you until you navigate to a different market.

### Shareable links

Your provider choice is reflected in the URL:

| Parameter | What it does |
|-----------|-------------|
| `?market=` | Selects the market within an event |
| `?tab=polymarket` or `?tab=dflow` | Locks the provider tab to a specific venue |

No `tab` parameter? Auto-routing decides. Select a different market and the `tab` clears, so routing re-evaluates from scratch.

## Provider access

| Your status | Where noma can route you |
|-------------|-------------------------|
| **No KYC** | Polymarket only |
| **KYC verified (via Proof)** | Polymarket + Kalshi — best price wins |

Without verification, every order goes to Polymarket. Complete KYC through Proof and noma unlocks Kalshi — comparing both orderbooks and routing to whichever has the better fill.

Kalshi markets require identity verification through [Proof](https://dflow.net/proof), a privacy-preserving KYC provider. This is a one-time process that takes about 2 minutes.

<Note>
**KYC only gates trading, not browsing.** You can view every market, every orderbook, and every price from every provider without verifying. See [Providers](/predict/providers) for the full breakdown.
</Note>
