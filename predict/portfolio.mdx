Your portfolio gives you a single view of every prediction you've made — across Polymarket, Kalshi, and Limitless — all in one place.

## Active positions

Your active positions show everything you need to manage open trades:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Market** | The prediction question |
| **Side** | Whether you hold Yes or No shares |
| **Shares** | Number of shares held |
| **Avg Price** | Your average entry price per share |
| **Current Price** | The market's current trading price |
| **P&L** | Your unrealized profit or loss |

Positions update in real time as market prices move.

## Cross-provider positions

Since noma aggregates markets from multiple providers, your portfolio may include positions from:

- **Polymarket** — Decentralized markets on Polygon
- **Kalshi** — Regulated markets via DFlow on Solana

All positions are displayed together — no switching between apps or tabs.

## Resolved positions

When a market resolves, your position moves to the **Resolved** section showing:
- Whether your prediction was correct
- Your payout amount (winning) or loss (losing)
- Transaction details for on-chain settlement

### Winning positions

Winning positions can be redeemed directly from the Resolved tab. Click **Redeem** to claim your payout — the settlement happens on-chain and your funds are returned to your wallet automatically.

### Closing lost positions

Lost positions on Solana (Kalshi markets via DFlow) will show a **Close** button in the Resolved tab. Closing a lost position is completely free for you — noma sponsors the transaction fee — and it helps the platform in a meaningful way.

**Why it matters:** Every trade on Solana creates a token account that locks up a small deposit (~$0.25) to keep the account open on-chain. When you close an expired position, that deposit is released back to noma, which we put directly toward continuing to sponsor gas fees for every user on the platform. The more positions that get closed, the longer we can keep trading completely free.

**How it works:**
1. Click **Close** on any lost position in your Resolved tab
2. Approve the transaction in your wallet (one signature)
3. That's it — the expired tokens are burned and the account is cleaned up

You lose nothing by closing — the tokens are already worthless after a loss. You're just helping clean up the on-chain state so noma can reinvest those resources into keeping the platform free for everyone.
