Your portfolio gives you a single view of every prediction you've made — across Polymarket, Kalshi, and Attest markets — all in one place.

## Active positions

Your active positions show everything you need to manage open trades:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Market** | The prediction question |
| **Side** | Whether you hold Yes or No shares |
| **Shares** | Number of shares held |
| **Avg Price** | Your average entry price per share |
| **Current Price** | The market's current trading price |
| **P&L** | Your unrealized profit or loss |

Positions update in real time as market prices move.

## Cross-provider positions

Since noma aggregates markets from multiple providers, your portfolio may include positions from:

- **Polymarket** — Decentralized markets on Polygon
- **Kalshi** — Regulated markets via DFlow on Solana
- **Attest** — Community-created markets on noma

All positions are displayed together — no switching between apps or tabs.

## Resolved positions

When a market resolves, your position moves to the **Resolved** section showing:
- Whether your prediction was correct
- Your payout amount (winning) or loss (losing)
- Transaction details for on-chain settlement

Winning payouts are automatic — you don't need to manually redeem them.

## Balance overview

Your current balance is shown in the top-right corner of the app. Click it to see:
- Your Trading Safe address (Polygon)
- Your Solana wallet address
- Quick access to the deposit modal
