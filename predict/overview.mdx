Predict is noma's core trading interface. Browse live prediction markets on stocks, crypto, and commodities — all powered by real liquidity from Polymarket and other sources.

## How it works

1. **Browse markets** — Filter by asset category (crypto, equities, commodities) or search by name
2. **Check the odds** — Each market shows the current Yes probability, 24h volume, and time remaining
3. **Trade** — Buy Yes or No shares at the current price or set a limit order
4. **Collect** — When the market resolves, winning shares pay out $1.00 each

## Market types

### Price predictions
The most common market type. A specific asset price target with a deadline:

> "Bitcoin above $95,000 by March 20?"

### Up or Down (5-minute markets)
Ultra-short-duration markets that resolve every 5 minutes:

> "Bitcoin Up or Down — March 12, 3:00PM–3:05PM ET"

These markets resolve automatically using on-chain price feeds (Chainlink oracles for crypto).

### Event-based
Markets on real-world events from Polymarket's catalog:

> "Will the Fed cut rates in March?"

## Supported assets

noma supports prediction markets on a wide range of assets. See [Supported Assets](/predict/supported-assets) for the full list.

## Fees

noma charges **zero trading fees**. You pay only the spread between bid and ask prices set by the market.
