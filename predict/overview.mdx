This is where you trade. Every market from every provider, one feed. Filter by asset, check the odds, and buy Yes or No shares — noma routes you to the best price automatically.

## How it works

1. **Browse markets** — Filter by asset (BTC, ETH, AAPL, Gold), by category (crypto, stocks, events, politics), or search by name. Markets come from Polymarket, Kalshi, and other providers — all in one feed.
2. **Check the odds** — Each market shows the current Yes/No probability, 24h volume, time remaining, and which providers offer it. When a market exists on multiple venues, you'll see provider badges.
3. **Trade** — Buy Yes or No shares at the current price or set a limit order. Zero platform fees, gas covered on both Polygon and Solana. Noma routes to the best price when multiple providers have the same market.
4. **Collect and climb** — Winning shares pay out \$1.00 each, automatically. Every resolved prediction updates your Noma ID — win rate, leaderboard ranking, prediction streak, and badges.

## Market types

### Price predictions
A specific asset price target with a deadline:

> "Bitcoin above \$95,000 by March 20?"

Buy **Yes** if you think it will. Buy **No** if you don't. The share price reflects the market's implied probability — if Yes trades at \$0.65, the market implies a 65% chance.

### Up or Down (5-minute markets)
Ultra-short markets that resolve every 5 minutes:

> "Bitcoin Up or Down — March 12, 3:00PM–3:05PM ET"

A new round starts every 5 minutes. Pick a direction, trade, and see the result in minutes. These resolve automatically using on-chain price feeds.

### Event-based
Markets on real-world events — politics, Fed decisions, tech launches, sports, and more:

> "Will the Fed cut rates in March?"

## What you can trade on

Crypto (BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP), equities (AAPL, NVDA, MSFT, GOOGL, AMZN, TSLA, META), commodities (Gold, Silver), politics, Fed decisions, sports, tech launches, and more. Polymarket alone has processed billions in cumulative trading volume; Kalshi covers hundreds of regulated event contracts. If it exists on any connected provider — it's on noma. See [Supported Assets](/predict/supported-assets) for the full list.
