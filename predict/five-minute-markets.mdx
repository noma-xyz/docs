title: "5-Minute Markets"
---

5-minute markets are the fastest way to build your prediction streak. A new round starts every 5 minutes, resolves automatically, and every result counts toward your track record.

## How they work

Each round asks one question:

> "Will [Asset] go **Up** or **Down** in the next 5 minutes?"

- **Up** — The price at the end of the window is higher than at the start
- **Down** — The price is lower or equal

Pick your side, trade, and see the result before your coffee gets cold.

## Lifecycle

```
Round opens → Trading window (5 min) → Price snapshot → Resolution
```

1. **Round opens** — A new market is created with a start price from the oracle
2. **Trading window** — Buy Up or Down shares while the round is active
3. **Price snapshot** — At the end of the window, the oracle records the closing price
4. **Resolution** — The market resolves instantly. Winning shares pay out, losing shares are burned

No waiting days or weeks. You get your result in minutes, and your next round is already live.

## Resolution oracles

5-minute markets use trusted, tamper-resistant price feeds:

| Asset | Oracle |
|-------|--------|
| BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP | Chainlink on-chain price feeds |
| Equities (AAPL, NVDA, etc.) | Yahoo Finance (during market hours) |

<Warning>
5-minute markets are fast and volatile. Prices can swing significantly within a single round. Only trade what you're comfortable losing.
</Warning>
