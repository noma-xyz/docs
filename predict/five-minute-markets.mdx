5-minute markets are fast-paced prediction rounds on crypto and stock prices. A new round starts every 5 minutes, and each one resolves automatically using on-chain price feeds.

## How they work

Each round asks a simple question:

> "Will [Asset] go **Up** or **Down** in the next 5 minutes?"

- **Up** means the price at the end of the window is higher than the price at the start
- **Down** means the price is lower or equal

## Lifecycle

```
Round opens → Trading window (5 min) → Price snapshot → Resolution
```

1. **Round opens** — A new market is created with a start price from the oracle
2. **Trading window** — Buy Yes (Up) or No (Down) shares while the round is active
3. **Price snapshot** — At the end of the window, the oracle records the closing price
4. **Resolution** — The market resolves instantly. Up shares pay out if the price went up, Down shares pay out if it went down.

## Resolution oracles

5-minute markets use trusted, tamper-resistant price feeds for resolution:

| Asset | Oracle |
|-------|--------|
| BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP | Chainlink on-chain price feeds |
| Equities (AAPL, NVDA, etc.) | Yahoo Finance (during market hours) |

<Warning>
5-minute markets are high-frequency and volatile. Prices can swing significantly within a single round. Only trade what you're comfortable losing.
</Warning>
