noma supports prediction markets across multiple asset classes. Markets are sourced from Polymarket and other providers.

## Crypto

| Asset | Ticker | Description |
|-------|--------|-------------|
| Bitcoin | BTC | The largest cryptocurrency by market cap |
| Ethereum | ETH | The leading smart contract platform |
| Solana | SOL | High-performance L1 blockchain |
| XRP | XRP | Cross-border payment network |

Strike prices and resolution use trusted data feeds:

| Asset Class | Primary Source | Fallback |
|-------------|---------------|----------|
| Crypto | Chainlink on-chain oracles | Binance, Bybit |
