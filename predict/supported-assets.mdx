Noma supports prediction markets across multiple asset classes. Markets are sourced from Polymarket, Kalshi, and other connected providers. If a market exists on any provider — it's tradeable on noma.

## Crypto

| Asset | Ticker | Market types | Providers |
|-------|--------|-------------|-----------|
| Bitcoin | BTC | Price threshold, Up/Down (5-min, 15-min), End-of-Day | Polymarket |
| Ethereum | ETH | Price threshold, Up/Down (5-min, 15-min), End-of-Day | Polymarket |
| Solana | SOL | Price threshold, Up/Down (5-min) | Polymarket |
| XRP | XRP | Price threshold, Up/Down (5-min) | Polymarket |

Crypto markets run 24/7. 5-minute rounds resolve automatically via Chainlink on-chain price feeds — a new round starts every 5 minutes with no downtime.

## Events & Politics

Beyond asset prices, noma surfaces event-based markets from both providers:

- **Politics** — Elections, policy decisions, government actions
- **Fed & macro** — Rate decisions, inflation, economic indicators
- **Sports** — Game outcomes, player props, tournament results
- **Tech** — Product launches, earnings, company milestones

Event markets are sourced from both Polymarket and Kalshi. Kalshi is CFTC-regulated and covers hundreds of event contracts. Polymarket is the largest on-chain prediction market, with billions in cumulative trading volume ([source](https://polymarket.com)).

## Price data sources

Strike prices and resolution use trusted, tamper-resistant data feeds:

| Asset Class | Primary Source | Fallback |
|-------------|---------------|----------|
| Crypto | Chainlink on-chain oracles | Binance, Bybit |
| Equities | Yahoo Finance | — |
| Commodities | Yahoo Finance | — |

Chainlink price feeds secure billions in value across DeFi and are the industry standard for on-chain price data ([source](https://chain.link)).
