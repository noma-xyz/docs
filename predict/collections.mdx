title: "Collections"
---

When you browse the Predict page, you'll notice that related markets are grouped together instead of listed individually. These groups are called **collections** — they give you a single page with a shared chart, timeline, and context for all the markets in a set.

## Round-based collections

These group short-duration markets (5-minute, 15-minute) for a specific asset — for example, all 5-minute BTC rounds.

When you open a round-based collection, you'll see:

- A **timeline selector** to navigate between rounds
- **Live price chart** with the strike price overlay
- **Countdown timer** for the current round
- Automatic rotation to the next round when the current one ends

<Tip>
Round-based collections auto-advance. When a round expires, the page automatically loads the next active round so you're always looking at the current market.
</Tip>

## Series collections

These group recurring events — NBA games, Fed meetings, earnings calls, or any market set that shares a common theme.

You'll see:

- An **event selector** to browse between events in the series
- **Market selector** to switch between individual markets within an event
- Standard chart and orderbook views

## Single-event collections

Some collections contain just one event with multiple markets. For example, an election event might have separate markets for each candidate. These show a market selector and a multi-probability chart.

## Page layout

Every collection page follows the same structure:

1. **Header** — Asset/event name, category badge, provider badges
2. **Selector** — Timeline (rounds) or event list (series)
3. **Market selector** — Switch between markets within the selected event
4. **Stats strip** — Volume, liquidity, time remaining
5. **Chart** — Price or probability over time
6. **Orderbook** — Live bids and asks
7. **Activity feed** — Recent trades

## Finding collections

Collections appear throughout the Predict page:

- **5-Minute Rounds** section at the top shows live round-based collections
- **Trending** sections include popular collections
- Browsing by asset category surfaces collections for that asset
- Clicking "View collection" on any market takes you to its parent collection
