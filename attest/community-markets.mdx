## The problem with curated markets

On platforms like Polymarket and Kalshi, a small team decides which markets get listed. That means:

- **Your question might never exist** — If the team doesn't think it's interesting enough, it won't get listed
- **Slow to react** — By the time a trending topic gets a market, the moment may have passed
- **Limited coverage** — Only a few hundred active markets at any given time, out of the infinite questions the world could trade on
- **No long-tail** — Niche communities, local events, and emerging narratives are ignored

## Noma's approach: let the community decide

With Attest, **anyone can create a prediction market**. No applications, no review boards, no gatekeepers. If you have a question the world should be trading on, you can make it happen.

The process is simple:

1. **You propose a question** with clear resolution criteria
2. **The community votes with their wallets** — if enough people back it, the market launches
3. **Everyone trades** on a live, liquid market

This is fully permissionless. There is no approval process, no curation committee, no editorial filter. If the crowd wants it, it ships.

## Why this matters

### Speed
Breaking news? Controversial prediction? You don't wait for a platform to decide — propose the market and let the community fund it in minutes.

### Coverage
Curated platforms have hundreds of markets. A permissionless system can have **thousands** — covering every asset, every event, every angle that any community cares about.

### Skin in the game
Backers aren't just voting on what's interesting — they're putting capital behind it. Markets that get funded are markets people actually want to trade. This is natural demand discovery.

### Community ownership
When you create and fund a market, you earn a share of the trading fees it generates. Market creators and early backers are rewarded for building the ecosystem.

## What makes a great community market?

The best community markets share a few traits:

- **Timely** — tied to something happening right now (earnings, launches, policy decisions)
- **Specific** — clear resolution criteria that leave no room for ambiguity
- **Opinionated** — people have strong views and want to put money behind them
- **Underserved** — the question doesn't exist on any curated platform
