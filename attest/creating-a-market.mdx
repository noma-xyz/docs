## Step-by-step

### 1. Define your question

Your question must be:
- **Binary** — resolvable as Yes or No
- **Specific** — include a clear target, asset, and deadline
- **Verifiable** — the outcome must be objectively determinable

<Note>
**Good:** "Will Bitcoin be above $100,000 on June 1, 2026?"

**Bad:** "Will Bitcoin do well this year?" (too vague, not verifiable)
</Note>

### 2. Set resolution criteria

Choose how the market will resolve:
- **Price feed** — Automatically resolves using on-chain oracles (best for asset price questions)
- **UMA Oracle** — Decentralized resolution for subjective or event-based questions

### 3. Set a deadline

Every market needs an end date. This is when resolution occurs and winning shares pay out.

### 4. Submit for funding

Once submitted, your market enters the **funding phase**. It needs to reach the funding threshold before it can go live.

## Funding thresholds

| Tier | Threshold | Best for |
|------|-----------|----------|
| Micro | $500 | Niche or experimental questions |
| Standard | $1,000 | Most prediction markets |
| Large | $2,500+ | High-interest events |

## What happens after funding?

Once the threshold is met:
1. An AMM (Automated Market Maker) is deployed with the funded liquidity
2. The market appears on the Predict page for anyone to trade
3. Early backers earn fee-share rewards from trading activity

## Tips for successful markets

- **Be specific** — Clear resolution criteria attract more backers
- **Pick trending topics** — Markets on current events fund faster
- **Set reasonable deadlines** — Too far out reduces urgency; too soon limits trading time
