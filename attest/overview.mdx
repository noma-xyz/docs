## What is Attest?

Attest is noma's upcoming system for permissionless prediction markets. It will allow anyone to propose, fund, and trade custom markets on any topic.

### What's coming

- **Create any market** — Propose a prediction on any topic with custom resolution criteria
- **Crowdfunded liquidity** — Back markets you believe in to earn a share of trading fees
- **Community resolution** — Decentralized settlement through staked validators

Attest is currently in development. Join our [Discord](https://discord.gg/nomaxyz) to stay updated on the launch.
