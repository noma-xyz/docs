Attest is Noma's core differentiator — a fully permissionless system for creating prediction markets. Anyone can propose a question, the community funds it with real capital, and once the funding threshold is met, the market goes live for trading. No applications, no approvals, no gatekeepers.

**This is what sets Noma apart.** While other platforms curate a limited set of markets behind closed doors, Noma lets the community decide what's worth trading on. If you have a question the world should be pricing, you can make it happen — in minutes, not weeks.

## Why Attest?

Centralized prediction markets curate a limited set of ~500 markets. If you want to trade on a question that doesn't exist yet — say, "Will AAPL hit $250 before Q3 earnings?" — you're out of luck.

Attest changes that. You propose the question, the crowd funds it, and the market launches permissionlessly on an automated market maker (AMM). Creators and early backers earn fee-share rewards from the trading activity their market generates.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Propose">
    Create a structured claim — a yes/no question with clear resolution criteria and a deadline. Example: "ETH above $5,000 by December 31, 2026."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fund">
    The community backs the market with USDC. Backers choose a side (Yes, No, or Neutral) and earn fee-share rewards when the market goes live.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Trade">
    Once the funding threshold is hit, the market launches with an AMM (Automated Market Maker). Anyone can trade Yes/No shares.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Resolve">
    At the deadline, the market resolves. Auto-resolvable markets (price feeds) settle instantly. Others use UMA's optimistic oracle for decentralized resolution.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Market categories

Attest supports structured question types:

| Type | Example |
|------|---------|
| **Price threshold** | "BTC above $100,000 by June 1?" |
| **Up/Down** | "Will ETH go up or down this week?" |
| **Range** | "AAPL between $180–$200 at earnings?" |
| **Event** | "Will the Fed cut rates in Q2?" |

## Resolution

Markets resolve through one of three mechanisms depending on the question type:

1. **Auto-resolution** — Price feed markets resolve automatically using Chainlink oracles
2. **Source-verified** — Markets with clear, verifiable data sources (e.g., earnings reports)
3. **UMA Oracle** — Subjective or complex questions use [UMA's optimistic oracle](https://docs.uma.xyz/protocol-overview/how-does-umas-oracle-work) for decentralized resolution
