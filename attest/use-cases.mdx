Attest lets anyone create a prediction market. Here are some of the most compelling use cases — and the kinds of markets the community is building.

## Crypto price predictions

The most popular category. Traders create markets on specific price targets for any supported asset:

- "BTC above $150,000 by September 1?"
- "SOL to $500 before ETH hits $10,000?"
- "NVDA stock price above $200 at Q3 earnings?"

These markets resolve automatically using on-chain price feeds (Chainlink oracles for crypto, Yahoo Finance for equities), so there's no dispute risk.

## Earnings & financial events

Create markets around corporate earnings, economic data releases, and financial events:

- "Will Apple report revenue above $100B in Q2?"
- "Fed funds rate below 4% by December?"
- "Tesla deliveries above 500K in Q3?"

These use verifiable data sources for resolution — earnings reports, government releases, official filings.

## Tech & product launches

The crypto and tech community moves fast. Create markets on launches, announcements, and milestones:

- "Will Ethereum ship Pectra upgrade before June?"
- "Apple to announce AR glasses at WWDC?"
- "Solana TPS above 100K sustained for a week?"

## Politics & policy

Prediction markets have a strong track record on political outcomes. Create markets on elections, legislation, and policy decisions:

- "Will the infrastructure bill pass the Senate this session?"
- "Next Supreme Court retirement before 2027?"
- "EU AI Act enforcement action in 2026?"

## Sports & culture

Not everything has to be finance. Create markets on cultural events, sports, and entertainment:

- "Will the Chiefs make the Super Bowl again?"
- "Box office opening weekend above $200M for the next Marvel film?"
- "Billboard #1 song on July 4th?"

## Niche & community-specific

This is where permissionless creation really shines. Markets that would never exist on a curated platform:

- "Will the Ethereum Foundation treasury fall below $500M?"
- "GitHub stars for [project] above 10K by year end?"
- "Will the largest NFT collection floor price recover to 10 ETH?"

The long tail is where community markets add the most value — questions that matter deeply to specific communities but aren't "big enough" for a curated platform.

## Tips for market creators

<Note>
**Start with what you know.** The best markets come from people with domain expertise who understand what questions their community wants to trade on.
</Note>

1. **Be specific** — "BTC above $100K by June 1" is better than "Will BTC go up?"
2. **Choose verifiable resolution** — Use price feeds or official data sources whenever possible
3. **Time it right** — Create markets when interest is peaking (before earnings, during events)
4. **Share it** — Post your market in Pulses and on social media to attract backers
5. **Back it yourself** — Show conviction by being one of the first backers
