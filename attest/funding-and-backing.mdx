title: "Funding & Backing"
---

## Backing a market

When someone proposes a new market, it needs community funding before it can go live. As a backer, you contribute USDC and choose a side:

| Side | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Yes** | You believe the outcome will be Yes — your funds seed the Yes side of the AMM |
| **No** | You believe the outcome will be No — your funds seed the No side |
| **Neutral** | No directional bet — your funds provide balanced liquidity to both sides |

## Early backer incentives

Backers who fund early get better rewards:

| Funding Stage | Fee Multiplier |
|---------------|---------------|
| First 25% of threshold | **2.0x** fee share |
| 25–50% | **1.5x** fee share |
| 50–75% | **1.25x** fee share |
| 75–100% | **1.0x** fee share |

The earlier you back a market, the larger your share of trading fees once it goes live.

## What backers earn

Once a market is live, backers earn a share of the trading fees generated by the AMM. Your earnings depend on:

1. **How early you backed** — earlier backers get a multiplier on their fee share
2. **How much you contributed** — larger stakes earn proportionally more
3. **Trading volume** — more trading activity means more fees to distribute

## What if the market doesn't fund?

If a market doesn't reach its funding threshold by the deadline, all backer contributions are returned in full. No risk if the market doesn't launch.

## Withdrawing backing

You can withdraw your backing at any time before the market reaches its funding threshold. Once the threshold is met and the market goes live, your funds are locked in the AMM until the market resolves.
