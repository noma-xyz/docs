## What's live

- **Predict** — Trade on crypto, equity, and commodity price predictions with real orderbooks
- **5-minute markets** — Ultra-short prediction rounds with auto-resolution via Chainlink
- **Multi-provider aggregation** — Polymarket, Kalshi, and Limitless markets in one interface
- **Matching engine** — AI-powered cross-provider market linking with best execution routing
- **Rewards** — Points, streaks, referrals, and volume multipliers
- **Pulses** — Social boards for curating markets and competing with friends
- **Leaderboards** — Global, Pulse, and Fund rankings
- **Gasless trading** — Gasless trading on Polygon, Solana, and Base
- **Kalshi trading** — Full order execution on Kalshi markets via DFlow (requires KYC)
- **Limitless Exchange** — 500+ markets on Base with gas-sponsored trading, no KYC required
- **For You feed** — Personalized market discovery powered by social signals, vector similarity, and trading history
- **Multi-auth** — Sign in with wallet, email, Twitter, or Discord
- **Collections** — Grouped market views for rounds, series, and events
