Noma is actively building. Here's what's on the horizon.

## Live now

- **Predict** — Trade on crypto, equity, and commodity price predictions
- **5-minute markets** — Ultra-short prediction rounds with auto-resolution
- **Rewards** — Points, streaks, referrals, and volume multipliers
- **Pulses** — Social boards for curating markets with friends
- **Leaderboards** — Global and group-based rankings
- **Gasless trading** — Zero gas fees on Polygon
- **Multi-auth** — Sign in with wallet, email, Twitter, or Discord

## Coming soon

- **Attest** — Permissionless, crowdfunded prediction markets
- **Funds** — Permissionless prediction market pools with fund managers
- **Kalshi integration** — CFTC-regulated markets via DFlow on Solana
- **Track** — Follow top traders and copy their predictions
- **Public API & SDK** — TypeScript and Python SDKs for building on Noma
- **Mobile app** — Native mobile experience

## Future

- **Auto-copy trading** — Automated trade copying with budgets and filters
- **Additional market providers** — Hyperliquid, and more
- **Advanced analytics** — Historical performance, market correlations, and portfolio insights
- **Challenges** — Time-limited trading competitions with prizes

<Note>
This roadmap reflects current plans and priorities. Features and timelines may change. Follow us on [X](https://x.com) and join our [Discord](https://discord.gg) for the latest updates.
</Note>
