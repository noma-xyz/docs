<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Noma?">
    Noma is a prediction market platform that brings together markets from Polymarket, Kalshi, and Limitless Exchange into a single interface. You get zero trading fees, gasless execution, best-price routing across all three venues, and a public identity that tracks your predictions. See [Why Noma?](/why-noma) for the full picture.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How is noma different from Polymarket or Kalshi?">
    Polymarket, Kalshi, and Limitless are individual prediction market platforms. Noma aggregates all three into one interface — so you see every market, get the best price across venues, and never pay trading fees or gas. On top of that, noma adds a social and identity layer (Pulses, leaderboards, Noma ID, Funds) that doesn't exist on any single platform. See the [comparison table](/why-noma#every-market-best-price-zero-fees).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do prediction markets work?">
    Prediction markets let you buy Yes or No shares on future events. Share prices range from \$0.01 to \$0.99 and reflect the market's implied probability — a \$0.65 Yes share means the market implies a 65% chance. When a market resolves, winning shares pay out \$1.00 and losing shares pay \$0.00. See [How Prediction Markets Work](/how-prediction-markets-work) for a detailed explanation.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What assets can I trade on?">
    Crypto (BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP), equities (AAPL, NVDA, MSFT, GOOGL, AMZN, TSLA, META), and commodities (Gold, Silver). New assets are added regularly based on community demand. See [Supported Assets](/predict/supported-assets) for the full list.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are there any fees?">
    Noma charges zero trading fees on every trade, on every venue. Gas fees on Polygon, Solana, and Base are also covered by noma — you never need to hold MATIC, SOL, or ETH. You only pay the bid-ask spread set by the market. Note that some providers charge their own taker fees on select market types (e.g., Polymarket charges taker fees on certain crypto and sports markets) — these are provider fees, not noma fees.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is gasless trading?">
    Noma covers all transaction fees on Polygon, Solana, and Base. When you place a trade, you sign a message and noma's relayer submits the transaction on-chain and pays the gas. This applies to trading, order cancellation, wallet setup, and payout redemption. You only need USDC to trade — nothing else. See [Wallet Setup](/wallet/setup) for details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I sign in?">
    Click Sign In and choose from wallet (MetaMask, Phantom, etc.), email, Twitter, or Discord. Noma creates a secure, non-custodial wallet for you automatically — no seed phrases, no browser extensions, no friction.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I deposit funds?">
    Click the **Deposit** button on the dashboard. For Polymarket markets, deposit USDC.e to your Trading Safe on Polygon. For Kalshi markets, deposit USDC on Solana. You can buy crypto directly with a card via the built-in Coinbase Onramp, or use Blink to connect your browser wallet and bridge funds automatically. See [Account Funding](/predict/account-funding) for the full walkthrough.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is a Noma ID?">
    Your Noma ID is your unique identity on the platform — it replaces your wallet address on leaderboards, Pulses, Funds, and badges. Claiming one gives you a public profile at `noma.xyz/id`, a permanent +0.05x reward multiplier, and the Noma ID badge. See [Noma ID](/social/noma-id).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What are Pulses?">
    Pulses are social groups where you and your friends pin markets, see each other's positions, and compete on a group leaderboard. Private Pulses are invite-only; public Pulses are open to anyone. See [Pulses](/social/pulses).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is Attest?">
    Attest is noma's upcoming permissionless market creation system. It will allow anyone to propose, fund, and trade custom prediction markets. Coming soon.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What are Funds?">
    Funds are permissionless prediction market pools where a manager trades on behalf of depositors. Use one solo to build a public, on-chain track record — or pool capital with friends. All trades are transparent. See [Funds](/funds/overview) for details and risk disclosures.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is Noma custodial?">
    No. Noma is fully non-custodial. Your wallet keys are managed by Privy and never leave your device. Your Trading Safe is a smart contract only you control. Noma cannot access, move, or freeze your funds.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need KYC?">
    Not for Polymarket or Limitless markets — you can trade immediately after signing in. KYC (via Proof) is only required for Kalshi markets, because Kalshi is a CFTC-regulated exchange. Without KYC, you can still browse all markets from all providers — you just can't place orders on Kalshi-sourced markets. See [Providers](/predict/providers).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is noma safe?">
    Yes. Noma is fully non-custodial — your wallet keys are managed by Privy and never leave your device. Your trading funds sit in a Gnosis Safe smart contract that only you can authorize. Noma cannot access, move, or freeze your funds. All trades settle on-chain on Polygon, Solana, and Base and are verifiable on their respective block explorers. See [Security](/resources/security) for details on the architecture.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I withdraw from noma?">
    Withdrawals are handled directly from your Trading Safe (Polygon) or Solana wallet. You can withdraw your full balance at any time — noma never locks your funds. On Polygon, withdrawals are sent as USDC.e to any wallet address you specify.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is a prediction market platform?">
    A prediction market platform lets you trade on future events by buying Yes or No shares. Noma combines markets from multiple providers (Polymarket, Kalshi, and Limitless) into a single interface — so instead of checking multiple apps and managing multiple wallets, you see every market in one place, compare prices across venues, and trade wherever the best price is. Noma also adds a social and identity layer on top.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Noma vs Polymarket — what's the difference?">
    Polymarket is a single prediction market platform. Noma aggregates Polymarket, Kalshi, and Limitless into one interface — so you see every market, get best-price routing across all three venues, and pay zero trading fees. Noma also adds features that don't exist on Polymarket: Noma ID (public identity), Pulses (social trading groups), Funds (managed prediction pools), leaderboards, badges, and reward multipliers. If you already trade on Polymarket, noma connects to the same orderbook — same markets, same liquidity — but adds an identity and social layer on top. See the full [comparison table](/why-noma#every-market-best-price-zero-fees).
  </Accordion>
