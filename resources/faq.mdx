<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Noma?">
    Noma is a prediction market aggregator that brings together markets from Polymarket, Kalshi, and other providers into a single interface. You can trade on asset prices, create your own markets, earn rewards, and compete with friends — all with zero trading fees.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do prediction markets work?">
    Prediction markets let you buy Yes or No shares on future events. Share prices range from $0.01 to $0.99 and reflect the market's implied probability. When a market resolves, winning shares pay out $1.00 and losing shares pay $0.00. See [How Prediction Markets Work](/how-prediction-markets-work) for a detailed explanation.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What assets can I trade on?">
    Noma supports prediction markets on crypto (BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP), equities (AAPL, NVDA, MSFT, GOOGL, AMZN, TSLA, META), and commodities (Gold, Silver). New assets are added regularly. See [Supported Assets](/predict/supported-assets) for the full list.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I sign in?">
    Click Sign In and choose from wallet (MetaMask, Phantom, etc.), email, Twitter, or Discord. Noma uses Privy to create a secure embedded wallet for you automatically — no seed phrases or extensions needed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I deposit funds?">
    Click the + button in the header. For Polymarket markets, deposit USDC.e to your Trading Safe on Polygon. For Solana markets, deposit SOL to your Solana wallet. You can also buy crypto directly with a card via the built-in MoonPay on-ramp. See [Deposits](/wallet/deposits) for details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are there any trading fees?">
    No. Noma charges zero trading fees. You only pay the bid-ask spread set by the market. Gas fees on Polygon are also covered by Noma.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do rewards work?">
    Earn points through daily check-ins, prediction streaks, referrals, linking socials, and claiming your Noma ID. Your trading volume unlocks multiplier tiers that boost all point earnings. See [Rewards](/rewards/overview) for the full breakdown.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What are Attestations?">
    Attestations (Attest) let anyone create a prediction market. You propose a yes/no question, the community funds it with USDC, and once the funding threshold is hit, the market goes live for trading. See [Attest](/attest/overview) for details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How are markets resolved?">
    Resolution depends on the platform. Polymarket markets use UMA's optimistic oracle or auto-resolution via price feeds. Kalshi markets are resolved by Kalshi using official data sources. Noma Attest markets use Chainlink oracles or UMA. See [Resolution](/attest/resolution) for details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What are Funds?">
    Funds are permissionless prediction market pools where a manager trades on behalf of depositors. Anyone can create a Fund, and anyone can deposit. Funds are fully transparent — all trades are visible on-chain. See [Funds](/funds/overview) for details and risk disclosures.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is Noma custodial?">
    No. Noma is non-custodial. Your embedded wallet keys are managed by Privy and never leave your device. Your Trading Safe is a smart contract you control. Noma cannot access or move your funds.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What are keyboard shortcuts?">
    Press **?** to open the keyboard shortcuts panel. Key shortcuts include **/** for search, **+** for deposit, and **Shift+key** for navigation between pages.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
