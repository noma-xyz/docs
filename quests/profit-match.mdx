Profit Match doubles your winnings. In partnership with [Blink](https://blink.cash), every trade you win during the campaign is matched **1:1** — for every dollar of profit you make, we pay you another, up to **\$100 per user**.

Trade like you normally would. When a market resolves in your favor, your profit is matched and paid to your noma wallet in USDC.

## How it works

- **Matched 1:1.** Your profit on a winning trade is matched by an equal amount. Win \$20, get \$20 on top.
- **Up to \$100 per user.** Matches accrue across the campaign until you reach \$100 — that's the per-user cap.
- **Opened _and_ resolved in the window.** A trade only qualifies if you **open the position after the campaign starts** _and_ the market **resolves before the campaign ends**. Positions you held before the campaign, or that are still open when it ends, don't count.

## How to qualify

<Steps>
  <Step title="Deposit with Blink">
    One tap creates your Blink passkey, then deposit — Blink sponsors the bridge
    fee, so there's no extra cost to fund your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Trade during the campaign window">
    Open positions on any market after the campaign starts. Only trades opened
    inside the window are eligible.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Win">
    When a qualifying market resolves in your favor and is redeemed, your profit
    is matched automatically — there's nothing to claim.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Getting paid

Matched amounts are paid as **USDC to your Base wallet on noma**. Payouts run on a regular cadence: you'll receive your match **within 48 hours** of a qualifying market resolving. No claim step — it lands in your balance automatically.

## The campaign window

- **Start:** June 28 1PM ET.
- **End:** there's no fixed end date. Profit Match runs until we approach the program's capacity, at which point we'll wind it down — we'll give notice before it closes.
- Only trades that are **opened and resolved + redeemed** inside this window qualify for the match.

## In partnership with Blink

[Blink](https://blink.cash) powers the deposit experience behind Profit Match. Your Blink passkey and deposit qualify you for the campaign, and Blink sponsors the bridge fees on every deposit so funding your account is free.
