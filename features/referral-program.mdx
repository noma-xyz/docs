## How it works

Noma is built for communities. Every user gets a unique referral link — bring your friends in and both of you get rewarded:

- **You** earn **500 points**
- **They** earn **500 points**

More friends means more competition in your Pulse, more liquidity in your favorite markets, and more people to flex your track record on.

## Finding your referral link

1. Go to the **Rewards** page
2. Your referral link is displayed in the referral section
3. Click to copy or share directly to Twitter

## Sharing

You can share your referral link via:
- **Direct link** — Copy and paste anywhere
- **Twitter** — One-click share with a pre-filled tweet
- **Discord** — Share in your community channels

## Tracking

The Rewards page shows:
- Total referrals you've made
- Points earned from referrals
- Your all-time referral count

## Terms

- Referral points are credited when the referred user completes their first sign-in
- Self-referrals are not eligible
- Noma reserves the right to adjust the referral program
