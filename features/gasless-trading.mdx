---

Noma covers all gas fees on Polygon, so you never need to hold MATIC or worry about transaction costs.

## How it works

Noma uses a **relayer architecture** powered by Polymarket's Builder Relayer:

1. You sign a transaction with your wallet (a simple signature, not a gas-consuming transaction)
2. Noma's relayer submits the transaction on-chain and pays the gas fee
3. The transaction executes on Polygon and your trade is placed

This applies to:
- **Wallet setup** — Deploying your Trading Safe and setting approvals
- **Trading** — Placing and cancelling orders
- **Redemption** — Claiming payouts from resolved markets

## Why gasless?

Traditional DeFi requires users to hold the network's native token (MATIC for Polygon) to pay for transactions. This adds friction:
- New users need to acquire MATIC before they can do anything
- Gas prices fluctuate, making costs unpredictable
- Failed transactions still cost gas

Noma eliminates all of this. You only need USDC.e to trade.

## Security

Gasless doesn't mean trustless is compromised. Every transaction is:
- **Signed by you** — The relayer can only submit transactions you've authorized
- **Non-custodial** — Noma never has access to your funds
- **Verifiable** — All transactions are on-chain and can be verified on Polygonscan
