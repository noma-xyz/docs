description: "A unified view of all your positions across prediction market providers."
---

Noma gives you a single dashboard to track all your prediction market positions, regardless of which platform they originate from.

## What you see

Your portfolio shows:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Market** | The prediction question |
| **Side** | Whether you hold Yes or No shares |
| **Shares** | Number of shares held |
| **Avg Price** | Your average entry price per share |
| **Current Price** | The market's current trading price |
| **P&L** | Your unrealized profit or loss |
| **Status** | Active, resolved, or redeemable |

## Cross-platform positions

Since Noma aggregates markets from multiple sources, your portfolio may include positions from:

- **Polymarket** — Decentralized markets on Polygon
- **Kalshi** — Regulated markets via DFlow on Solana
- **Attest** — Community-created markets on Noma

All positions are displayed in a single unified view.

## Resolved positions

When a market resolves, your positions move to the **Resolved** section showing:
- Whether your prediction was correct
- Your payout amount (winning) or loss (losing)
- Transaction details for on-chain settlements

## Balance overview

The header displays your current USDC balance across networks. Click the balance to see:
- Your Trading Safe address (Polygon)
- Your Solana wallet address
- Quick access to the deposit modal
