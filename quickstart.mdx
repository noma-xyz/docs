## 1. Sign in

Click **Sign In** in the top right corner. Noma supports multiple sign-in methods via [Privy](https://privy.io):

- **Wallet** — Connect MetaMask, Phantom, Coinbase Wallet, or any WalletConnect-compatible wallet
- **Email** — Sign in with your email address
- **Twitter** — Sign in with your X/Twitter account
- **Discord** — Sign in with your Discord account

When you sign in, Noma automatically creates a secure **embedded wallet** for you using Privy. This wallet is non-custodial — only you control the keys. No seed phrases to write down; your wallet is tied to your login method and secured by Privy's infrastructure.

## 2. Set up your trading account

After signing in, Noma automatically sets up a **Polymarket trading account** for you. This includes:

- **Deploying a Trading Safe** — A smart contract wallet (Gnosis Safe) on Polygon that holds your trading funds
- **Setting token approvals** — Permissions for the prediction market contracts to execute trades
- **Generating trading credentials** — Encrypted API keys for order placement

This process is **completely free** (Noma covers all gas fees), **automatic**, and takes about 30 seconds. You'll be prompted to sign 2–3 messages to authorize the setup — no transaction fees involved.

<Tip>You only need to do this once. After setup, your trading account is ready for all future sessions.</Tip>

## 3. Deposit funds

Click the **+** button in the header to open the deposit modal.

- **Polygon (USDC.e)** — For trading on Polymarket-powered markets. Funds go to your Trading Safe.
- **Solana (SOL)** — For Solana-based markets and future Kalshi integration.

You can deposit via QR code, direct transfer, or the built-in on-ramp to buy crypto with a card via MoonPay.

## 4. Place a prediction

1. Browse markets on the **Predict** page — filter by asset (BTC, ETH, AAPL, Gold, etc.)
2. Select a market (e.g., "Bitcoin above $90,000 by March 15?")
3. Choose **Yes** or **No**
4. Enter your stake amount
5. Confirm the trade

Your position appears immediately in your portfolio. If your prediction is correct when the market resolves, you receive $1.00 per share.

## 5. Earn rewards

Head to the **Rewards** page to:
- Complete your daily check-in for bonus points
- Build prediction streaks for increasing rewards
- Link your socials (Twitter, Discord, Email) for +150 points each
- Refer friends for 500 points per signup
