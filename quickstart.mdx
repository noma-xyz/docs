Four steps to your first prediction. Every one after that builds your public track record.

## 1. Sign in

Click **Sign In** in the top right corner. Pick whichever method is easiest for you:

- **Wallet** — MetaMask, Phantom, Coinbase Wallet, or any WalletConnect-compatible wallet
- **Email** — Just your email address
- **Twitter** — Your X/Twitter account
- **Discord** — Your Discord account

Noma automatically creates a secure, non-custodial wallet for you — no seed phrases, no browser extensions, no friction.

## 2. Set up your trading account

Noma handles this automatically. You'll see a few pop-up prompts asking you to approve — just click **Sign** each time. The whole process takes about 30 seconds, costs you nothing, and you only do it once.

<Tip>Curious what's happening behind the scenes? See [Wallet Setup](/wallet/setup) for the technical details.</Tip>

## 3. Deposit funds

Click the **+** button in the header to open the deposit modal.

Polymarket markets run on **Polygon** (USDC.e). Kalshi markets run on **Solana** (USDC) via DFlow. You can deposit via QR code, direct wallet transfer, or buy crypto with a card through the built-in MoonPay on-ramp.

<Note>
Noma handles chain routing automatically — you don't need to worry about which chain a market uses. See [Deposits](/wallet/deposits) for details on both networks.
</Note>

## 4. Place your first prediction

1. Browse markets on the **Predict** page — filter by asset (BTC, ETH, AAPL, Gold, etc.)
2. Pick a market (e.g., "Bitcoin above \$90,000 by March 15?")
3. Choose **Yes** or **No**
4. Enter your stake
5. Confirm

Your position appears immediately in your portfolio. If your prediction is correct when the market resolves, you receive \$1.00 per share. No fees taken.

## Now build your identity

You just placed your first prediction — and it's already part of your permanent, public track record on noma. Your Noma ID is recording it. The leaderboard sees you. Now keep going.

You'll earn more and climb faster if you set up your identity now:

- **Claim your Noma ID** — Your public identity at `noma.xyz/id`. Instant +500 points and a permanent +0.05x multiplier on everything you earn.
- **Daily check-in** — Visit the Rewards page each day to claim bonus points. Your streak grows the longer you keep it going.
- **Keep predicting** — Make at least one prediction daily to build your streak. At 30+ days, you're earning 50 pts/day — 1,500 points per month from streaks alone.
