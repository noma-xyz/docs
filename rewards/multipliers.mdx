## How multipliers work

The more you trade, the faster you earn. Your total multiplier is the sum of three components:

```
Total Multiplier = Base (1.0x) + Volume Tier (up to 1.0x) + Noma ID Bonus (0.05x)
```

The maximum multiplier is **2.05x**, which doubles your point earnings on every action.

## Volume tiers

Your volume tier is based on your total all-time trading volume on noma:

| Tier | Volume Required | Multiplier Bonus |
|------|----------------|-----------------|
| **Spectator** | \$0 | +0.0x |
| **Predictor** | \$100 | +0.25x |
| **Analyst** | \$1,000 | +0.50x |
| **Strategist** | \$5,000 | +0.75x |
| **Oracle** | \$25,000 | +1.0x |

Your tier is based on cumulative volume — it never resets.

## Noma ID bonus

Claiming your Noma ID (your unique ID with a profile at `noma.xyz/id`) adds a permanent **+0.05x** bonus to your multiplier. Claim yours on the Settings page.

## Example

A user at the **Analyst** tier (\$1,000+ volume) with a Noma ID:

```
Total = 1.0 (base) + 0.50 (Analyst) + 0.05 (Noma ID) = 1.55x
```

If they earn 25 points from a daily check-in, they actually receive:

```
25 × 1.55 = 38.75 → 39 points
```

## Checking your multiplier

Your current multiplier and progress to the next tier are shown on the **Rewards** page. The progress bar shows how close you are to unlocking the next volume tier.
