## Daily check-ins

Visit noma each day and claim your check-in. Points increase the longer your streak runs:

- **Days 1–6:** 5 points/day
- **Day 7 (weekly bonus):** 25 points

Missing a day resets your streak to zero. A 30-day streak earns 180 points total — but break it and you start from scratch.

## Prediction streaks

Place at least one prediction every day to keep your prediction streak alive. The longer you maintain it, the more you earn per day:

| Streak | Daily Bonus |
|--------|-------------|
| Days 1–6 | 15 pts/day |
| Day 7+ | 25 pts/day |
| Day 14+ | 35 pts/day |
| Day 30+ | 50 pts/day |

At 30+ days, you're earning 50 points per day just for staying active. That's 1,500 points per month from streaks alone — on top of everything else.

## Referrals

When someone signs up through your referral link:
- **You** earn 500 points
- **They** earn 500 points

Find your referral link on the **Rewards** page. Share it directly, or post to Twitter and Discord with one click. Your total referral count and points earned are tracked on the same page.

More friends on noma means more competition in your Pulse, more liquidity in your markets, and more people to climb the leaderboard against.

## Social linking

Connect Twitter, Discord, or Email for 150 points each. Linking 2 of 3 unlocks the **Connected** badge — visible on your profile and next to your name on leaderboards.

## Volume multipliers

Your trading volume unlocks multiplier tiers that boost **all** point earnings — check-ins, streaks, referrals, everything. The more you trade, the faster every other reward compounds.
