Noma rewards active participants with points that unlock badges, boost your leaderboard ranking, and track your standing across the community. The more you trade, share, and engage — the more you earn.

## How to earn

| Action | Points | Frequency |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| Daily check-in | 5–25 pts | Once per day |
| Prediction streak | 15–50 pts/day | Daily (grows at milestones) |
| Refer a friend | 500 pts | Per referral (both earn) |
| Link Twitter | 150 pts | One-time |
| Link Discord | 150 pts | One-time |
| Link Email | 150 pts | One-time |
| Claim Noma ID | 500 pts | One-time |

## Daily check-ins

Visit Nomaeach day and claim your daily check-in bonus. Points increase with your streak:

- **Days 1–6:** 5 points/day
- **Day 7 (weekly bonus):** 25 points

Missing a day resets your streak to zero, so consistency is key.

## Prediction streaks

Make at least one prediction each day to maintain your prediction streak. Streak rewards grow at milestone days:

| Streak | Daily Bonus |
|--------|-------------|
| Days 1–6 | 15 pts/day |
| Day 7+ | 25 pts/day |
| Day 14+ | 35 pts/day |
| Day 30+ | 50 pts/day |

## Referrals

Bring your friends. When someone signs up through your referral link:
- **You** earn 500 points
- **They** earn 500 points

The best traders bring competition. Find your referral link on the Rewards page and share it in your Pulse, on Twitter, or in Discord.

## Social linking

Connect your accounts for one-time point bonuses. Linking 2 of 3 accounts (Twitter, Discord, Email) unlocks the **Connected** badge — proof that you're a real person, not a bot.

## Volume multipliers

Your trading volume unlocks multiplier tiers that boost all point earnings. See [Multipliers](/rewards/multipliers) for the full breakdown.
