Your Noma ID is your prediction identity. It's how you show up on leaderboards, in Pulses, on Fund profiles, and anywhere your name appears on noma. It replaces your wallet address with something recognizable — and comes with a public profile that proves your track record.

## What you get

A Noma ID gives you a **public profile** at `noma.xyz/id` that displays:

- Your prediction track record — win rate, total return, streak history
- Every badge you've earned
- Your current reward multiplier and volume tier
- Fund performance (if you manage one)
- Leaderboard rankings across global, Pulse, and Fund boards

This is your reputation. It's verifiable, it's public, and it grows with every prediction you make.

## Why claim one

- **Recognition** — No more anonymous wallet addresses. Your Noma ID follows you everywhere on the platform — leaderboards, Pulses, Funds, and badges.
- **Noma ID badge** — Displayed next to your name so everyone knows you've claimed your identity.
- **500 points** — Instant reward on claim
- **\+0.05x permanent reward multiplier** — Stacks with your volume tier. Every point you earn gets boosted, forever.

## How to claim

1. Go to **Settings**
2. Click **Claim Noma ID**
3. Done — your ID is generated and linked to your account

Claiming is free, takes seconds, and is permanent. Once claimed, your Noma ID is yours — it can't be transferred, duplicated, or taken away.

## Where it appears

Your Noma ID shows up across the entire platform:

- **Leaderboards** — Global, Pulse, and Fund rankings
- **Pulses** — Activity feeds and consensus bars
- **Fund profiles** — As a manager or depositor
- **Badges** — Next to your achievements
- **Referral links** — Personalized with your identity
