Prediction markets are more fun when there's something to prove. Leaderboards rank traders by performance across three time periods: **weekly**, **monthly**, and **all-time**.

## Ranking criteria

Traders are ranked by a composite score based on:

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Profit** | Total realized P&L from resolved predictions |
| **Win rate** | Percentage of predictions that resolved in your favor |
| **Volume** | Total trading volume in USDC |

## Leaderboard types

### Global
The main leaderboard ranks all Noma traders. Top performers are highlighted on the Predict page and earn recognition across the platform. This is where you build your public reputation.

### Pulse
Each Pulse has its own leaderboard. See how you stack up against your friends without competing against the entire platform — bragging rights included.

### Funds
Fund leaderboards rank managed Funds by performance. Top Funds get visibility and attract more depositors.

## Noma IDs

Your **Noma ID** (e.g., NOMA-4291) is your public identity on the leaderboard. Claim yours on the Settings page — it also earns you a **+0.05x** reward multiplier.

## Tips for climbing

- **Consistency matters** — Win rate is weighted heavily; a steady 60% win rate beats occasional big wins
- **Volume unlocks tiers** — Higher volume tiers boost your reward multiplier, which improves your ranking
- **Diversify** — Spreading predictions across multiple markets reduces variance
- **Bring friends** — More competition in your Pulse means more motivation to stay sharp
- **Maintain streaks** — Daily prediction streaks earn bonus points that push you up the rankings
