Where do you rank? Leaderboards track every trader's performance and surface the best predictors on noma. The board is public — and so is your position on it.

## How you're ranked

Traders are ranked by a composite score across three metrics:

| Metric | What it measures |
|--------|-----------------|
| **Profit** | Total realized P&L from resolved predictions |
| **Win rate** | Percentage of predictions that resolved in your favor |
| **Volume** | Total trading volume in USDC |

Rankings update across three time periods: **weekly**, **monthly**, and **all-time**.

## Three leaderboards

### Global
The main board. Every noma trader, ranked by performance. Top performers get visibility across the platform — this is where you build your public reputation. Your Noma ID and badges are displayed next to your ranking.

### Pulse
Each Pulse has its own leaderboard. See how you stack up against your friends without competing against the entire platform. The stakes are lower, the trash talk is higher.

### Funds
Fund leaderboards rank managed Funds by performance. Top Funds get visibility and attract more depositors — a strong leaderboard position is the best marketing a Fund manager can have.

## How to climb

- **Consistency beats variance** — A steady 60% win rate climbs faster than occasional big wins followed by losses
- **Volume unlocks multipliers** — Higher volume tiers boost your reward multiplier, accelerating your point accumulation
- **Diversify** — Spreading predictions across markets reduces variance and smooths your track record
- **Maintain streaks** — Daily prediction streaks earn bonus points that push you up the rankings
- **Bring friends** — More competition in your Pulse means more motivation to stay sharp
