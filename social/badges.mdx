Badges are collectible achievements displayed on your Noma profile. They recognize milestones, activity, and contributions to the community — and give you something to show off to your Pulse.

## How to earn badges

Badges are awarded automatically when you complete specific actions or reach milestones:

| Badge | How to earn |
|-------|-------------|
| **Connected** | Link 2 of 3 social accounts (Twitter, Discord, Email) |
| **Noma ID** | Claim your unique Noma ID (NOMA-XXXX) |
| **First Prediction** | Place your first prediction on any market |
| **Streak** | Maintain a prediction streak for 7+ consecutive days |
| **Referrer** | Refer your first friend to Noma |
| **Early Adopter** | Sign up during Noma's early access period |

<Note>
New badges are added over time. Check the Rewards page for the latest available badges and your progress toward earning them.
</Note>

## Viewing badges

Your earned badges are displayed everywhere that matters:
- **Your profile** — Visible to other users and Pulse members
- **Leaderboards** — Badges appear next to your Noma ID so everyone can see your achievements
- **Rewards page** — See all available badges and track your progress toward earning them

## Badge benefits

Beyond recognition, some badges provide tangible benefits:
- **Noma ID badge** — Grants a permanent **+0.05x** reward multiplier
- **Connected badge** — Confirms your verified social identity

Badges are non-transferable and permanently tied to your account.
