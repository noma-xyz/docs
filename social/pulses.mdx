Pulses are the social layer of Noma — private or public groups where you and your friends curate prediction markets, see each other's positions, share takes, and compete on a group leaderboard. Prediction markets are better with friends.

## Creating a Pulse

1. Go to the **Friends** tab
2. Click **Create Pulse**
3. Set a name and optional description
4. Invite friends via share link or username

## Features

### Pinned markets
Pulse admins can pin markets for the whole group to follow. Pinned markets show a **group consensus bar** — a visual breakdown of how many members are bullish (Yes) vs bearish (No).

### Pulse leaderboard
See who's leading in your Pulse based on prediction accuracy and profit. Compete for bragging rights without risking shared capital — everyone trades individually.

### Activity feed
See what your Pulse members are trading. When someone in your Pulse places a prediction, it shows up in the feed.

## Privacy

- **Private Pulses** — Only visible to members. New members join via invite link.
- **Public Pulses** — Discoverable by anyone. Anyone can join.

Your individual positions and P&L are visible to Pulse members. If you prefer privacy, use a public Pulse where only aggregated consensus is shown.
