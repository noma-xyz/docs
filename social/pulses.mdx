Prediction markets hit different when your friends are on the other side of the trade.

Pulses are groups where you and your friends curate markets, see each other's positions, and compete on a private leaderboard. Pin a market, watch your friends take the opposite side, and settle it when the market resolves.

## Create a Pulse

1. Go to the **Friends** tab
2. Click **Create Pulse**
3. Set a name and optional description
4. Invite friends via share link or username

That's it. Your group is live.

## What Pulses give you

### Pinned markets
Pulse admins can pin markets for the whole group. Pinned markets show a **consensus bar** — a visual breakdown of how many members are bullish (Yes) vs bearish (No). See where your group stands before you take your position.

### Group leaderboard
See who's leading in your Pulse based on prediction accuracy and profit. Everyone trades individually with their own capital — the leaderboard is for bragging rights. And bragging rights matter.

### Activity feed
See what your Pulse members are trading in real time. When someone places a prediction, it shows up in your feed. Follow their moves, fade their takes, or pile on.

## Privacy

- **Private Pulses** — Only visible to members. Join via invite link.
- **Public Pulses** — Discoverable by anyone. Open to join.

Your positions and P&L are visible to Pulse members. This is what makes it social — the transparency creates competition.
