## The problem: fragmented markets

Prediction markets are split across multiple platforms, each with their own interface, liquidity, and rules:

- **Polymarket** — Largest decentralized prediction market, but curates a limited set of ~500 markets
- **Kalshi** — CFTC-regulated, but US-only and limited asset coverage
- **Others** — Scattered liquidity across smaller platforms

As a trader, you're forced to:
- Maintain accounts on multiple platforms
- Track positions across different interfaces
- Miss markets that exist on one platform but not another
- Accept whatever markets the platform chooses to list

## The solution: one interface, every market

Noma aggregates prediction markets from all major providers into a single interface. You get:

### Unified access
Browse and trade markets from Polymarket, Kalshi, and more — all in one place. One account, one portfolio, one interface.

### Permissionless market creation — Noma's biggest differentiator
Don't see a market you want to trade? **Create it yourself with Attest.** Propose a question, let the community fund it, and trade on the outcome. No gatekeepers, no applications, no editorial review. If the crowd wants it, it ships.

Curated platforms have hundreds of markets. Noma can have **thousands** — because the community decides what's worth trading on. Creators and early backers earn fee-share rewards from the markets they bring to life.

### Best execution
Noma routes your orders to the venue with the best price and deepest liquidity. You always get the best available execution across all connected platforms.

### Zero fees
Noma charges no trading fees. You only pay the spread between bid and ask prices set by the market.

### Instant onboarding
Sign in with your wallet, email, Twitter, or Discord. Noma creates an embedded wallet for you automatically — no seed phrases, no extensions, no friction. Your trading account is set up in seconds, completely free.

### Your predictions build your reputation
Every trade you make on Noma builds a public, verifiable track record. Run a **Fund** to prove your edge solo, or pool capital with friends and compete on the leaderboard. Your win rate, returns, and streaks are all on-chain — no hiding, no faking it.

### Social trading
Curate markets with friends in **Pulses**, compete on **Leaderboards**, and follow top traders. Prediction markets are better with friends.

## Who is Noma for?

- **Traders who want an edge** — One interface, best execution across Polymarket, Kalshi, and more
- **Market creators** — Create prediction markets on questions that don't exist yet, and earn from them
- **Reputation builders** — Prove your track record on-chain with Funds, badges, and leaderboard rankings
- **Friend groups** — Pool capital, compete in Pulses, and trade together
