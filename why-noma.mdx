You make a great call on Bitcoin. You nail an earnings prediction. You build a 30-day streak of winning trades. And then — nothing. Your track record disappears into a dashboard no one will ever see.

Noma changes that. Every prediction builds a **public, verifiable reputation** — your Noma ID, badges, leaderboard ranking, and Fund performance. A track record anyone can see, and no one can fake.

## Your identity grows with every trade

This is what makes noma different from a trading app.

- **Noma ID** — Your unique identity with a public profile at `noma.xyz/id`. Displayed on leaderboards, in Pulses, on Fund profiles, and next to every badge you earn. This is your reputation.
- **Funds** — Build a transparent, on-chain track record. Win rate, total return, drawdowns — all verifiable and permanently yours. As your record grows, others can deposit and follow your trades.
- **Badges** — Earn achievements for prediction streaks, volume milestones, social connections, and market creation. Displayed next to your name everywhere.
- **Leaderboards** — Compete globally, within your Pulse, and across Funds. Weekly, monthly, and all-time.

Other platforms give you a trade history you can't share. Noma gives you an identity you can prove.

## Trade with friends

Prediction markets are better when you have people to compete with.

- **Pulses** — Create a private group, pin markets, see each other's positions, and compete on a group leaderboard. When your friend buys Yes and you buy No — now it's personal.
- **Funds** — Pool capital with friends, pick a manager, and trade as a crew. Every trade is transparent, every return is public.
- **Referrals** — Bring friends in. You both earn 500 points. More friends means more competition in your Pulse and more people to flex your track record on.

## Every market. Best price. Zero fees.

Your identity is built on top of real trading infrastructure. ### Best execution

When the same market exists on multiple providers, noma compares orderbooks and routes you to the venue with the better price. If you try to trade on the worse-priced venue, a confirmation modal tells you a better price exists. If you have funds on one chain but not the other, noma defaults to where you can actually trade. You're always free to choose — noma just makes sure you see the full picture.

## Already on Polymarket or Kalshi?

You don't switch — you upgrade. Start trading through noma and every trade builds your Noma ID, leaderboard ranking, and prediction streak from day one. Same markets, same liquidity, same settlement — noma connects to each provider's orderbook directly. You're adding an identity layer, social features, cross-provider routing, and zero trading fees on top of the platforms you already know.

noma uses its own wallet infrastructure, so previous trade history from other platforms won't carry over. You're starting fresh — but every position from here is yours.

## Who uses noma

**Short-term traders** — 5-minute markets resolve before your coffee gets cold. Trade BTC, ETH, or AAPL up/down rounds all day, and every win counts toward your streak.

**Politics and macro junkies** — You called the last rate decision before the market priced it in. On noma, you can trade on Fed decisions, elections, and policy changes — and prove you saw it coming.

**Friend groups** — Create a Pulse, pin a market, and see who's actually willing to put money where their mouth is. The group leaderboard settles every debate.

**Fund managers** — Build a transparent, on-chain track record that speaks for itself. As your win rate grows, others can deposit into your Fund and follow your trades.
