## Your profile

Your noma profile is your public-facing identity. It shows your track record, badges, and activity to other users across the platform.

To view or edit your profile, click your avatar in the top-right corner and select **Settings**.

### What's on your profile

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Noma ID** | Your unique identifier (claim one if you haven't yet) |
| **Avatar** | Your profile picture, pulled from your connected social account |
| **Badges** | Achievements you've earned |
| **Win rate** | Percentage of resolved predictions in your favor |
| **Total return** | Cumulative P&L across all markets |
| **Volume tier** | Your current multiplier tier based on trading volume |

## Linked accounts

Connect your social accounts for one-time point bonuses and to verify your identity:

| Account | Bonus | Benefit |
|---------|-------|---------|
| **Twitter** | 150 pts | Social verification, share trades |
| **Discord** | 150 pts | Community access, social verification |
| **Email** | 150 pts | Account recovery, notifications |

Linking 2 of 3 accounts unlocks the **Connected** badge — proof that you're a real person, not a bot.

To link accounts, go to **Settings** and click **Connect** next to each provider.

## Connected wallets

Your wallet connections are managed through Privy:

- **Embedded wallet** — Created automatically when you sign up. This is your primary wallet.
- **External wallets** — You can connect additional wallets (MetaMask, Phantom, etc.) for sign-in convenience.

Your Trading Safe on Polygon is linked to your embedded wallet and cannot be changed.

## Notifications

Configure what you want to be notified about:

- Market resolution alerts
- Pulse activity
- Referral signups
- Streak reminders

## Danger zone

- **Sign out** — Ends your current session. Your wallet and positions are safe — you can sign back in anytime.
- **Note:** Noma accounts cannot be deleted while you have open positions or active Fund deposits.
