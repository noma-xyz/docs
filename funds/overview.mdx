Traditional hedge funds charge 2% management + 20% performance fees, require accredited investor status, and lock up capital for months. On noma, anyone can create a Fund, set their own fees, and every trade is visible on-chain. No gatekeepers, no lockups, no minimum investment.

Run one solo to prove your edge, or pool capital with friends and manage together. Either way, every trade is transparent, every return is public, and your reputation grows with every prediction.

## Two ways to use Funds

### Solo — prove you're not just lucky
Create a Fund for yourself. Every prediction you make builds a public, verifiable record — win rate, total return, max drawdown, all on-chain. As your performance grows, others can choose to deposit and follow your trades.

### With friends — trade as a crew
Start a Fund with your group. Pool capital, pick a manager (or rotate), and compete against other Funds on the leaderboard. Share it in your Pulse so everyone can track performance together.

<Warning>
Funds are **fully permissionless** — anyone can create one, and anyone can deposit. There is no curation, vetting, or approval process. Always do your own research on a fund manager's track record before depositing. See [Risks](#risks) below for full disclosures.
</Warning>

## How Funds work

1. **Create a Fund** — Any Noma user can spin one up. Set a name, strategy, and fee structure
2. **Trade solo or invite others** — Keep it personal or share the link in your Pulse, on Discord, or with friends
3. **The manager trades** — Place predictions using the Fund's capital
4. **Returns are tracked** — Performance is calculated automatically and visible to everyone. If you have depositors, profits and losses are shared proportionally

## Key properties

| Property | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Permissionless** | Anyone can create a Fund. No approval, no applications — just deploy and go. |
| **Transparent** | All trades are visible on-chain. Your depositors can see every move. |
| **Non-custodial** | Funds are managed via smart contracts. The manager can trade but cannot withdraw depositor funds. |
| **Social** | Share your Fund in Pulses, compete on leaderboards, and build your reputation as a manager. |

## Social features

Funds are deeply integrated with the rest of Noma:

- **Fund leaderboards** — Funds are ranked by performance. Top-performing Funds get visibility across the platform
- **Pulse integration** — Share your Fund in a Pulse so your group can track performance together
- **Manager profiles** — Fund managers build public track records visible to the entire community
- **Badges** — Creating and managing a successful Fund can earn you exclusive badges

## Fees

Fund managers can set:

| Fee Type | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Management fee** | Annual fee on assets under management (e.g., 2%) |
| **Performance fee** | Fee on profits above a high-water mark (e.g., 20%) |

Fee structures are set at Fund creation and displayed prominently. Review them before depositing.

## Risks

<Warning>
**Important risk disclosures:**

- Fund managers are **not vetted** by Noma. Anyone can create a Fund.
- Past performance does **not** guarantee future results.
- You can **lose some or all** of your deposited capital.
- Smart contract risk exists — Funds rely on on-chain contracts.
- **Never deposit more than you can afford to lose.**
</Warning>

## Evaluating a Fund

Before depositing, review:
- **Manager's track record** — Win rate, total return, and trading history on their Noma profile
- **Strategy description** — Does the manager clearly explain their approach?
- **Fee structure** — Are fees reasonable for the strategy?
- **Community reputation** — What do other traders say? Check the manager's badges and leaderboard ranking
- **Drawdown history** — How has the Fund performed during losing streaks?
