Funds let you build a transparent, on-chain track record — solo or with friends. Create a Fund, trade with its capital, and every prediction is publicly verifiable: win rate, total return, drawdowns, all on-chain. As your record grows, others can deposit and follow your trades.

## How it will work

1. **Create a Fund** — Set a name, strategy, and fee structure. No approval needed.
2. **Trade solo or invite others** — Keep it personal or share the link with friends.
3. **The manager trades** — Place predictions using the Fund's capital.
4. **Returns are tracked** — Performance is calculated automatically and visible to everyone.

## Two ways to use Funds

**Solo — prove you're not just lucky.** Every prediction builds a public, verifiable record. As your performance grows, others can deposit and follow your trades.

**With friends — trade as a crew.** Pool capital, pick a manager, and compete against other Funds on the leaderboard.
