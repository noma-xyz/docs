<Warning>
Fund creation is **permissionless** — there is no application or approval process. If you accept depositors, trade responsibly and communicate your strategy clearly.
</Warning>

## Creating a Fund

1. Navigate to the **Funds** section
2. Click **Create Fund**
3. Configure your Fund:
   - **Name** — Your trading identity or group name
   - **Description** — Explain your strategy (even for solo Funds — others can see it)
   - **Management fee** — Annual fee on AUM (optional, set to 0 for solo Funds)
   - **Performance fee** — Fee on profits above the high-water mark (optional)
4. Deploy the Fund contract — gasless, takes seconds

## Solo or social — your call

### Going solo
Use a Fund as your personal track record. Every prediction builds your public profile — win rate, total return, drawdowns, all verifiable on-chain. As your reputation grows, others may choose to deposit and follow your trades.

### With friends
Share your Fund and trade as a group:

- **Drop it in your Pulse** — Your friends see it instantly
- **Share the link** — Direct link to your Fund's page
- **Post on socials** — Twitter and Discord sharing built in

The more depositors, the larger the pool, the more markets you can diversify across.

## Managing your Fund

Once your Fund is live:

- **Trade** — Place predictions using the Fund's capital
- **Build your reputation** — Your Fund is ranked on the leaderboard. Every win adds to your public track record
- **Grow** — A strong track record attracts depositors organically
- **Earn badges** — Hit milestones to unlock manager-specific badges

## Best practices

- **Start solo or with friends** — Build a track record first, then open it up
- **Be transparent** — Clearly describe your strategy and risk approach
- **Stay active** — Consistent trading builds trust and reputation
- **Diversify** — Don't concentrate the Fund's capital in a single market
- **Show conviction** — Deposit your own capital alongside your depositors

## Fund metrics

Your Fund's public profile displays:

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **NAV** | Net asset value — total Fund value |
| **Total return** | All-time percentage return |
| **Win rate** | Percentage of winning predictions |
| **Max drawdown** | Largest peak-to-trough decline |
| **Depositors** | Number of active depositors |
| **AUM** | Assets under management |

These metrics are calculated automatically and visible to the entire Noma community. Top-performing Funds get featured on the platform.
